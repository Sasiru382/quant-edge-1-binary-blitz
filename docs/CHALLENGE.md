# Official requirement summary

Source: official competitor PDF attached to the organiser email forwarded on 3 October 2026. This is a paraphrased checklist, not an amendment to the organiser rules. Refer to the official PDF for authority; private email and team registration identifiers are not published.

- Combine copulas and wavelets in a portfolio market-risk framework.
- Investigate whether tail dependence changes with investment horizon and the risk consequences of ignoring it.
- Portfolio, assets, markets, period, risk measure and models are team decisions; justify all of them.
- Test out of sample against a simple benchmark.
- End with one actionable risk-manager recommendation.
- Any public data and any tools are allowed; computational submission language is Python or R.
- Every line of code and claim must be explainable. Unreproducible results are unsupported.
- Report: PDF ≤10 pages including figures/tables, excluding cover/references.
- Runnable code, README, dependency list and data/public download script.
- One command reproduces every report number and figure.
- One ZIP/team ≤25 MB, plus accessible Google Drive folder link in the portal.
- Deadline: 7 October 2026, 23:59 Sri Lanka time; no late marking.
- Disclose material AI assistance in README/appendix. Live explanation/modification/reproduction may be required next round.
- Screening selects 20 of 40 registered teams; judging is expected to take approximately 3–4 days after the deadline.

No frontend, hosted service or dashboard requirement appears in the supplied brief.
